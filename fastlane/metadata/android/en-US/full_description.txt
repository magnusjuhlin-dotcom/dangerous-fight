Swipe, slash and topple your opponent's tower in Dangerous Fight – a fast neon samurai duel in an arena of cracked stone and glowing lava!

⚔️ HOW TO PLAY
Swipe anywhere on the screen in the direction you want to dash. The further you pull, the harder your samurai flies. Crash into your opponent at full speed for slash damage, or ram the tower for heavy tower damage. Stand still in your charge zone to charge sword waves and fire them with one tap. Dash into an enemy shot and it bounces straight back! Destroy the other tower within four minutes to win.

🔥 SAMURAI RAGE AND COMBOS
Knock out, ram, parry and hit to fill the RAGE meter. When it glows, unleash twelve sword waves in every direction and deal double damage. Hits in quick succession build combos and bonus points.

🏯 1000 LEVELS
Every 50 waves you reach a new level with its own stone floor – and it keeps getting harder. The computer gets faster and tougher, and the arena fills up with stone pillars, fire vents that burst into flame and rolling boulders. Choose Easy, Normal or Hard yourself.

👹 BOSSES
Every other match you face a giant Shogun boss. Below half health it goes into a rage, glows red and slams the ground with shockwaves.

🎮 GAME MODES
• Versus the computer – with Cyber-Perks, levels and bosses
• Online – face the next player searching, or a friend with a room code
• Team matches 2v2, 3v3 and 4v4 – online, with friends or against the computer
• 2 players on the same phone

🥷 SAMURAI AND SHOPS
Unlock Cyber Ronin, Armored Shogun, Shadow Ninja and Oni Berserker in the Samurai Dojo. Buy new sword waves, upgrade your tower and recruit an extra samurai who jumps in mid-match – all with Cyber-Credits you earn by playing.
